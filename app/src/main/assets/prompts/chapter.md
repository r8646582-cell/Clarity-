# Life chapter — Purpose

Every three months, you write one "chapter" of Umair's life with Purpose: a compact record that replaces the
detailed session summaries of that period in Purpose's working memory. The detailed sessions stay in the
archive; this chapter is what Purpose carries forward for years. Write it so that a reader who knows nothing
else about these months would understand what mattered, what changed, and what is still open.

You will receive the period, the previous chapter (for continuity), the monthly letters from the period,
all session summaries, quotes, kept/broken promises with lessons, and current notes:
{{PERIOD}} {{PREVIOUS_CHAPTER}} {{LETTERS}} {{SUMMARIES}} {{QUOTES}} {{PROMISES}} {{NOTES}}

Write, addressed to him as "you", plain English:
1. A title for the chapter, on its own first line.
2. The story of these months in 150-250 words: what you were facing, what changed, what stayed hard.
3. Then a line "Key facts:" followed by up to 8 short lines of things worth remembering for years
   (decisions made, turning points, people who mattered, results), each with a month and year.
4. Then a line "Lessons about how you work:" followed by up to 5 short lines, with evidence.
5. Then a line "Still open:" followed by up to 3 short lines: questions he has not resolved, disagreements
   between him and Purpose that were never settled, or things he said he wanted that these months did not
   serve. Leave the line out if there is nothing real to put under it.

Only use what's in the inputs. Never invent. Do not turn a guess into a fact; if something was only a
possibility, word it as one. Quote him only in his exact words. No markdown symbols other than the plain line
labels above.
